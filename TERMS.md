IssueReady is a free Forge app, built for the Codegeist 2026 hackathon. It
is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

**What it does.** The app scores Jira issues 0–100 on data quality
(completeness, ownership, planning, labels, freshness, linkage), shows the
debt on the project page and in the issue panel, and offers one-click fixes.

**Your responsibility.** Fixes only run when you click them. Review the
proposed fix before applying it; the developer is not liable for changes
you approved, or for any loss from using the app.

**Acceptable use.** Use the app only on Jira sites you are allowed to
administer or use. Do not use it to collect, copy or disclose other
people's data outside your site.

repository is the current one.
