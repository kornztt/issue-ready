# Privacy Policy — IssueReady

Last updated: 2026-11-18.

IssueReady is a Forge app that runs entirely on Atlassian's cloud. It does
not send your data to any server outside Atlassian.

**What the app reads.** To compute a data-quality score, the app reads the
Jira issues in the project you audit: summary, description, status, assignee,
priority, labels, components, due date, estimates, links and update dates.

**What the app stores.** Scores, findings and audit history are stored in
Atlassian Forge storage, inside your own Jira site. Nothing is stored
anywhere else, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising. There
is no tracking and no third-party analytics.

**What the app changes.** The app only modifies an issue when you click a
fix: setting a missing priority, normalising label casing, or posting an
audit summary as a comment. It never deletes issues and never changes
anything on its own, except the weekly scheduled re-audit, which only
records a new snapshot.

**Deletion.** Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage, including
audit history, from your site.

**Contact.** Questions or issues: https://github.com/kornztt/issue-ready
